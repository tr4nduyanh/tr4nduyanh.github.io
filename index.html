The Digital Age Tortoise

The Digital Age Tortoise is the philosophy of FPT Software’s Computer Talents Club. Everything online moves fast; the tortoise is a reminder that understanding usually doesn’t. It stands for four habits I try to keep.

  • PatienceStaying with a hard problem before looking at someone else’s method.
  • Independent reasoningAttempting a problem on my own first, the change that shaped my IMOCSEA preparation.
  • ReflectionRecording approaches that failed and comparing strategies after trying.
  • Steady improvementNational Round Bronze, then International Round Silver.
